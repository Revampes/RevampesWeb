About Me

Who am I?

Hello there! I'm Revampes — a first-year Computer Science student diving headfirst into the world of code. Back in secondary school I picked biology, chemistry, and ICT as electives, and that curiosity for how tech intersects with other fields still fuels me today. I spend most of my free time messing around with code — Python, C++, Java, JavaScript, Rust, HTML and CSS — and I'm always eager to get even better. Right now I'm focused on leveling up my Python scripting and C++ skills. Outside of tech, I love balancing screen time with creativity and movement: sketching, painting, and pixel art for fun, plus I'm a total sports enthusiast — squash, tennis, basketball, table tennis, badminton... let's just say I'm always down to try a new one. This portfolio website is one of my current projects — I'm building it bit by bit, just like I'm building my skills. Thanks for stopping by to learn a little about me. Feel free to explore my projects and reach out if you want to connect!

Why I’m building it: First, it’s a hands-on way to practice the coding skills. I’m learning (like HTML&CSS and JavaScript) and turn theory into something real. And finally, it’s a chance to connect: if someone stumbles on this page who loves code, art, or sports as much as I do? That’s the best part. I’m building it bit by bit, just like I’m building my skills—no rush, just growth.
